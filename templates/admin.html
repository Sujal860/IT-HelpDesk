Modelling note

These scheduling policies model support requests as jobs in a shared service queue. Round Robin is a simulation of time-sliced scheduling; it does not require technicians to interrupt real customer work at fixed intervals. Working hours are 08:00–19:00; RR execution slices are split at the workday boundary.
