How aging works

Priority 1 is highest and 4 is lowest. Every 5 minutes a waiting ticket gains one priority level (its number decreases), down to priority 1. The scheduler rechecks effective priorities whenever the current process finishes. Each process runs to completion once started.

Important modelling note

This is an OS scheduling simulation for help-desk jobs, not the computer's real CPU scheduler. Running it updates estimates but does not mark tickets as resolved or assign a real technician. Priority aging is simulated at 5-minute working-time intervals. Work is scheduled only from 08:00 to 19:00; a ticket that cannot finish before 19:00 moves to the next day at 08:00.
